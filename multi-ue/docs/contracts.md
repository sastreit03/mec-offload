Descriptor byte offsets: 0 version(u8), 1 flags(u8), 2 reserved(u16), 4 UUID(16), 20 media_epoch(u64), 28 frame_id(u64), 36 gop_id(u64), 44 config_version(u32), 48 plan_version(u32), 52 frame_type(u8: IDR1/P3; all other values invalid), 53 padding(3), 56 encoded_bytes(u64), 64 capture_mono_ns, 72 tx_mono_ns, 80 capture_unix_ns, 88 tx_unix_ns. Flags bits0–3 identify the four timestamp validities. All integers are network-order; reserved/padding bits must be zero.

Routine defaults: MTU1200; jitter20ms with visible loss events; pending8 decoded frames/video and64MiB/video; encoded/decoded AU budget8MiB; receiver assembly64 frames; media staging1024 events; replay pages16; control queue128 with control above results; recorder16384; final-packet grace0.5s, receive-loss declaration1s, first-media timeout5s, drain5s, disconnect grace5s, snapshot5s, tracker retention1s and margin10ms. Numerical budgets/noise/warmup/accuracy are measured inputs. Probe defaults: twelve200ms intervals,1Mbps while on,1s lead,0.4s settling,correlation≥0.8,margin≥0.15,receipt≥20000B,30s lease,reprobe in final10s. These probe values are configurable experimental defaults, not established accuracy/recovery guarantees.
